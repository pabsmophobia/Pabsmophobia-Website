**An Analysis of Common Anomalies in Paranormal Photography**

I recently captured some photos in a dark interior that feature common anomalies often mistaken for paranormal "orbs." By analyzing these sequential shots and close-ups, we can see exactly why these features are almost always caused by **photographic artifacts**—not spirits.

**Part 1: Classic Flash Backscatter (The Moving Orb)**

The most compelling "orb" photos are often those taken just seconds apart, where a bright sphere appears and disappears. This is the **textbook definition of flash backscatter.**

**Part 2: Distant Spots and Digital Noise**

Sometimes, spots appear in very dark areas away from the camera. These are explained by distance and how the camera sensor works in low light.

- **Photo:**


![](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/11755a_8ae768ce61aa4b61b71545f4ca804161~mv2.png/v1/fill/w_49,h_27,al_c,q_85,usm_0.66_1.00_0.01,blur_2,enc_avif,quality_auto/11755a_8ae768ce61aa4b61b71545f4ca804161~mv2.png)

(Zoomed area to the right of the TV in Image 1)

- **Observation:** Two very faint, small spots appear when we zoom heavily into the dark recessed wall section.

- **Explanation:** Since these spots are far away, they are likely either **very distant dust particles** barely illuminated by the flash, or more commonly, **digital noise**. When you zoom into a dark area of a low-light photo, the camera's sensor noise and image compression artifacts often become exaggerated, appearing as random, faint bright spots.


**Part 3: Lens Flare (Reflections from Bright Light)**

Circular light anomalies can also be caused by intense light sources bouncing _inside_ the camera's lens elements.

**Photo:**

![](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/11755a_2239b97ccaec4ccc9522364b98023807~mv2.jpg/v1/fill/w_111,h_148,al_c,q_80,usm_0.66_1.00_0.01,blur_2,enc_avif,quality_auto/11755a_2239b97ccaec4ccc9522364b98023807~mv2.jpg)

- **Observation:** A faint, circular haze is visible on the far wall/door frame, opposite the intense red light source on the left.

- **Explanation:** This is a classic **lens flare.** The powerful red light on the left side of the frame enters the lens and reflects off internal surfaces, creating a "ghost image" (the circle) on the opposite side of the image sensor. This is a common optical effect, not a spirit.


**💡 Final Conclusion for Ghost Orbs**

While the appearance of a sphere of light in a dark place is exciting, virtually all "ghost orbs" can be conclusively explained by three non-paranormal causes:

1. **Flash Backscatter:** Dust or moisture reflecting the camera's light source.

2. **Digital Noise/Artifacts:** Exaggerated pixels in dark, zoomed-in areas.

3. **Lens Flare:** Internal reflection from a bright light source.


To seek genuine evidence, focus on anomalies that defy these well-documented physical and photographic principles!
