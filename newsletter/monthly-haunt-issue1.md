## 🏰 2. Venue Spotlight: The Ancient Ram Inn

Our most famous local venue is **The Ancient Ram Inn** in Wotton-under-Edge.

Dating back to 1145 and built on an ancient ley line intersection, it remains one of the UK's premier hotbeds for paranormal research. The venue manages and hosts its own lineup of in-house events, offering intimate vigils and guided historical tours led by their internal team.
